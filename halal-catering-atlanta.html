FAQ

Common questions

Is everything you cater halal?

Yes. Every protein at both kitchens is halal, for every event. There is no separate line and nothing to verify.

Do you cater mosque and community events?

Yes, regularly — across Buckhead, Duluth and the wider metro area.

How far ahead should we book?

Three to five days is comfortable. Larger buffets are easier with two weeks. Inside 72 hours, call us.

Do you serve alcohol or cook with it?

No. We don't serve alcohol and we don't cook with it.
