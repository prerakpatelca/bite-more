FAQ

Questions we get

Is Bite More halal?

Yes. Every protein at both locations is 100% halal — there is no separate halal menu because there is no non-halal menu.

What kind of food is Bite More?

Halal Italian-American. Pasta, smash burgers, wings and loaded fries, cooked to order at our locations.

Where is Bite More located?

Two locations: 3150 Roswell Rd NW, Suite A1 in Buckhead, Atlanta, and 2148 Duluth Hwy 120, Suite 117 in Duluth, GA.

What are your hours?

Buckhead: 11am–12am Mon–Wed, 11am–1am Thursday, 11am–3am Friday and Saturday, 11am–1am Sunday. Duluth: 11am–9pm Mon–Wed, 11am–11pm Thu–Sun.

Do you deliver?

Pickup and delivery are available through our ordering page. Enter your address there to check delivery availability and fees.

Do you cater?

Yes. Contact Bite More with your guest count, event date and location to discuss catering options.
