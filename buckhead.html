Around us

Where we sit
in Buckhead

We're on Roswell Road between Peachtree Road and Piedmont Road, at the edge of Buckhead Village. The Buckhead Theatre is a couple of minutes away, and Lenox Square and Phipps Plaza are a short drive north.

Before or after a show: the theatre lets out late and most kitchens nearby have stopped cooking. Ours runs to 3am Friday and Saturday, 1am Thursday and Sunday.

Parking: free lot parking on site — you don't need to circle Roswell Road looking for a space.

Neighbourhoods closest to us: Buckhead Village, Garden Hills, Peachtree Park, Peachtree Hills, Pine Hills, South Tuxedo Park and Lindbergh.
