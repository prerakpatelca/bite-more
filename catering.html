FAQ

Catering FAQ

How much notice do you need?

Three to five days is comfortable for most events. Larger buffets are easier with two weeks. Inside 72 hours, call us — we can usually still make it work.

Is the catering menu fully halal?

Yes. Every protein we serve is halal, at both locations and for every event, so there is nothing to flag to your guests.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes — delivered hot in chafing setups, laid out and labeled, with plates, utensils and serving spoons.

Can we build a custom menu?

Yes, and most larger bookings do. We cook from scratch, so if you want something we don't run daily, ask and we'll price it.

What about vegetarian guests?

Alfredo Pasta, mac and cheese, garlic toast and our fries are all meat-free, and buffet packages include a vegetarian tray.

Do you cater to Duluth office parks?

Yes. Our Duluth kitchen covers the Duluth Hwy 120 and Peachtree Industrial office parks, Johns Creek, Suwanee and Peachtree Corners.
