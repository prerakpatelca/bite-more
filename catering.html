FAQ

Catering questions

How many guests do you cater for?

From your group or more. Team Pasta Spread, Italian Spread, Executive Italian and Premium Italian Spread all serve 10. Boardroom starts at 10 individually packaged meals. Crowd Feast is built for 25 or more and Big Bite Feast for 50 or more.

Is everything halal?

Yes. Every protein we cater is halal, at our locations and for every event, so one menu covers the whole room.

What are the vegetarian options?

Spicy Vodka Rigatoni and Porcini Truffle Rigatoni on the pasta side; Sicilian Pesto and Calabrian on the Schiacciata side. Salads and sides are meat-free.

Can you do individually packaged meals?

Yes — that's the Boardroom package. Each guest gets a packaged entrée, individual dessert, beverage option, utensils and an item and dietary label. Guest name labels on request.

What's the difference between Executive Italian and Premium Italian Spread?

Executive Italian is about elevated ingredients, sparkling beverages and presentation. Premium Italian Spread is about more food and more variety.

How much notice do you need?

Current prices and availability are on the location-specific ordering menu. Review all checkout fees before ordering.

How is it priced?

Per package and per guest, quoted to your headcount. Send a date and number of guests and we'll come back with a menu and a price.
