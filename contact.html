FAQ

Common questions

What are your hours?

Buckhead: 11am–12am Mon–Wed, 11am–1am Thursday, 11am–3am Friday and Saturday, 11am–1am Sunday. Duluth: 11am–9pm Mon–Wed, 11am–11pm Thu–Sun.

Do you take large group bookings?

For groups over ten, call ahead so we can prep. Over twenty, catering is usually the better fit.

How do I book catering?

Send a date and headcount through the catering page and we'll come back with a menu and per-person price the same day.

Is there parking?

Yes, both locations have lot parking on site.
