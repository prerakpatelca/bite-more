What fully halal means

Some restaurants offer a halal item alongside non-halal dishes cooked on shared equipment. That's a different thing. Every protein we buy is halal, so there is no 'halal option' — there's just the menu.

What we cook

Italian-American. Cajun Alfredo and Alfredo pasta, smash burgers, wings baked then fried, loaded fries, Nashville hot chicken and slow-cooked birria.

Where to find us

FAQ

Halal questions

Do you serve pork?

No. There is no pork anywhere on our menu, at either location.

Do you serve or cook with alcohol?

No. We don't serve alcohol and we don't cook with it — no wine in the sauces, no beer batter.

Is the fryer shared with non-halal food?

No. Every protein in our kitchens is halal, so there is nothing non-halal to share equipment with.

Where does your meat come from?

We source halal from approved suppliers for both kitchens. Ask us in store and we'll tell you exactly who supplies what.
